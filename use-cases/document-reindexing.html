Use case

Refresh and re-index document backlogs without turning it into another pipeline project.

Fast Corpus supports async reprocessing of clean PDFs and text archives into AI-ready JSONL, chunks, embeddings, stable IDs, and a run report with a visible price basis and European execution context.

Fit

Best when the work is batch-oriented and retryable.

Document re-indexing fits Solvyr when the input can be split into bounded tasks, the job can tolerate async delivery, and the buyer wants predictable cost per usable output rather than realtime serving. It is a natural fit for archives and public teams that need AI-ready output while keeping European oversight visible.
