# Solvyr website V2 concept

Status: non-binding design and copy exploration. This directory is deliberately
separate from the production routes and is excluded from indexing. It has not
been deployed.

## Intent

- Preserve the Fast Corpus offer and the existing five-part information model:
  overview, fit, output, execution model, security, and pilot.
- Move the first impression from infrastructure to a bounded document backlog,
  accepted AI-ready corpus output, and inspectable run evidence.
- Extend the provisional merge-arrow metaphor: many interrupted inputs converge
  into one resolved output with a distinct dot.
- Use blue-to-violet only for orchestration, active paths, important actions,
  and the provisional mark; keep the page primarily white, cool grey, and navy.

## Provisional assets and treatments

The V2 now uses the exact working B Hybrid assets and colour tokens from
`solvyr-knowledge/pitch/concepts/solvyr_merge_arrow_v0_1/`. They remain concept
assets rather than a replacement for the production identity:

- full-colour B Hybrid logo in the header;
- reversed B Hybrid logo in the footer;
- B Hybrid icon as the V2 favicon;
- exact Ink, Night, navy, blue, violet, proof, and neutral tokens;
- interrupted input and resolved-output line primitives;
- light orchestration process treatment;
- dark animated workload-to-output illustration;
- dark approved-node map with organisation, external, specialised local, and
  central capacity classes;
- output-dot, node, status, section-line, card-accent, and footer treatments;
- product evidence frame based on the measured 100-PDF proof note.

The founder portraits embedded in the working Google Slides deck were copied
into `v2/assets/` for this concept at Jan's direction. The founder names, roles,
and LinkedIn links follow the same deck source register. No production logo,
favicon, app icon, social card, or other production brand asset is replaced.

## Evidence and claim boundaries

The visible proof fields come from the repository's 100-PDF internal run:
100 PDFs, 2,052 pages, 215 seconds wall-clock, 0.281 GPU-hours, 987 chunks,
about 1.3M estimated tokens, zero failures, and zero retries. The nearby copy
keeps the clean-PDF scope and does not extend that evidence to OCR-heavy scans,
layout parsing, or structured extraction.

The concept preserves the published EUR 0.95 per 1000 pages starting rate for
standard clean processing and keeps pilot/service scope separate.

The node map is a strategic execution-direction illustration, not a map of live
capacity. Partner- and customer-controlled workers remain a later, explicitly
approved path rather than current broad availability.
