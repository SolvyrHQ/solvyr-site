For introducers

Know a team with document work to improve?

Introduce us to a software company, integrator or organisation that wants to process documents with AI. A concrete task and a few examples are enough for a first conversation.

A useful starting point

Extract orders or invoices, enrich information, prepare a document archive or check a catalogue.

A reason to test

The team wants better output, less review work, lower processing costs or a clear agreement on processing location.

Suggested introduction

Keep it concrete.

“Solvyr helps run document work with AI. With a few examples, you can test output quality and processing costs for your process. Is there a document task where that would be useful?”

After the introduction

We take the test forward with the team.

We discuss the documents, intended result and processing setup. The partner keeps the customer relationship and workflow. Own hardware is optional.
